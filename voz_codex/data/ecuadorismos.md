# Compatibility copy

This file keeps pre-FREE-HANDS local tests and plugins working. The canonical
glossary is `free_hands/data/ecuadorismos.md`.

- **ñaño**: hermano, hermana, or a close friend.
- **pana**: close friend.
- **bacán**: very good or excellent.
- **pilas**: alert or quick-minded.
- **chuta**: colloquial surprise or concern.
- **acolitar**: help or give someone a hand.
- **chance**: a short while or opportunity.
- **de una**: immediately or with confidence.
- **full**: a colloquial intensifier.
